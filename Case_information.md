# UBS Hackathon Context

## Hackathon Format

This is a UBS hackathon.

Build time:
- 3 hours

Final presentation:
- 3-minute presentation/demo
- 2-minute Q&A

Important:
- Teams must clearly declare which parts of the code/demo are mocked or stubbed.

---

# Scenario 1 — Ticket Intelligence and Theme Detection

## Title

Ticket Intelligence and Theme Detection

## Tagline

From noise to insight: helping risk teams understand what's really going wrong.

---

# The Challenge

Risk and operations teams receive high volumes of:
- incidents
- escalations
- operational-risk issues

Pattern identification is still manual and slow.

---

# What We Need to Build

Create a lightweight AI prototype that:

- clusters tickets into meaningful themes
- detects emerging patterns and recurring issues
- surfaces potential root causes
- explains the key insights to a risk/operations user

---

# Expected Demo Output

Show a user going from:

raw tickets
→ identified themes
→ emerging patterns
→ actionable insights

Examples given by UBS:

- "Spike in login failures after a mobile update"
- "Recurring FX issue in APAC"

---

# Judging

There are four judging criteria.

Each criterion:
- 0–10 points

Maximum total:
- 40 points

---

## 1. Wow Factor

Judged on:
- originality
- clarity of presentation
- quality of the end-to-end demo

---

## 2. Technology Innovation

Judged on:
- effective use of AI
- creative use of technology to solve the challenge

---

## 3. Impact

Judged on:
- relevance and value to UBS
- potential user/business impact
- applicability

---

## 4. Feasibility & Scalability

Judged on:
- realistic approach
- awareness of constraints
- credible path to implementation
- credible path to scale

---

# What Good Looks Like

For Ticket Intelligence:

"Turns tickets into themes, patterns and explainable insights."

---

# Hard Constraints

When designing the solution, keep in mind:

- only 3 hours are available to build
- the final demo is only 3 minutes long
- the solution should be a lightweight prototype
- mocked/stubbed parts must be declared
- the end-to-end demo matters
- the prototype should use AI meaningfully
- the solution should be relevant to UBS risk/operations users
- the solution should have a credible implementation/scaling story
- insights should be explainable to the user

---

# Goal for Brainstorming

Use the information above as the fixed challenge context.

Do not assume a specific architecture or solution yet.

Brainstorm possible:
- user workflows
- product ideas
- AI approaches
- technical architectures
- UX concepts
- ways to detect patterns
- ways to surface root causes
- explainability approaches
- demo concepts
- differentiators
- ways to score well against all four judging criteria

Prioritise ideas that can realistically be prototyped within 3 hours.
